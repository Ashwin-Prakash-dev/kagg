## Image selection and background policy

- Images containing at least one ship: **6,935**
- Ship-free images available in FAIR1M: **17,840**
- Ship-free images retained: **771** (`--background-fraction 0.1`, seed 42)
- Total images in the derived dataset: **7,706**

### Why keep any negatives at all

A detector trained only on images that are guaranteed to contain a ship learns a biased prior and produces false positives on empty water and on land clutter. Retaining a slice of ship-free imagery gives the model explicit evidence about what is *not* a ship, which is what keeps precision meaningful.

### Why not keep all of them

Retaining all 17,840 would make the dataset 2.6x larger for the resolution sweep, multiply the Colab upload by roughly the same factor, and push the positive:negative image ratio to about 1:2.6. Most of that imagery is urban, airport or farmland scenery with no water in it, so the marginal negative teaches very little beyond the first few hundred.

The chosen 10% sits in the range Ultralytics recommends for background imagery and keeps the sweep affordable. The value is a flag, not a constant: `--background-fraction 0.0` reproduces a positives-only dataset and `1.0` retains every negative.

### Selection is fixed across all four resolutions

Sampling is seeded (`numpy.default_rng(42)`) and stratified by split. `image_manifest.csv` is written once and is the single input to every resolution condition, so E00-E03 see byte-identical image and annotation identities. This is the Part D control; it is enforced by construction rather than by convention.

### Caveat

These negatives are *easy* negatives -- predominantly land scenes rather than open water or harbours without vessels. They will suppress obvious false positives but should not be read as a hard-negative mining strategy.

## Split

The official FAIR1M Train/Val split is used unchanged.

| Split | Images | Ships | Background images |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Train | 4,930 | 31,462 | 522 |
| Val | 2,776 | 27,520 | 249 |

**Note for the write-up:** the official split is not balanced for ships. 47% of all ship instances fall in Val, even though Val holds only 36% of the images -- Val tiles are markedly denser in ships. That is a property of FAIR1M, not of this pipeline. The split is kept as-is because it is the published one and because the resolution comparison only requires that the split be *identical* across E00-E03, which it is. It should be stated as a limitation rather than silently re-split.

## Annotation cleaning

Rows flagged unusable by `scripts/validate_annotations.py` are excluded. For ships that exclusion is empty -- none of the 297 degenerate FAIR1M polygons is a ship -- so this layer contains every ship annotation in the dataset. See `data/annotation_validation/cleaning_report.md`.
